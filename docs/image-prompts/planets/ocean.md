```text
Subject: Ocean.
Intended use: planet map bodies and detail portraits.

Create a single isolated spherical planet with deep turquoise and blue global oceans, sparse small islands, a few cloud systems and a thin blue atmosphere. Front view of a true sphere, diameter approximately 76% of the square canvas. Consistent lighting from UPPER LEFT, natural terminator shadow toward lower right. Painterly realistic surface texture with the restrained blue-green and amber game palette where appropriate. The attached star is only a reference for organic painted surface richness: the planet must not emit light or look like plasma. No cities, buildings, spacecraft, moons, rings or star field.
Background: true transparent PNG with clean alpha, including between structures. No scenery or colored background. Keep the complete silhouette inside the frame with at least 12% clear padding.
Deliver one asset, suggested filename: ocean-planet.png. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, interface elements, borders or hex grid.
```
