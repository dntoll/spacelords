Stilreferenser: [Fighter](../references/fighter.webp), [Exploration ship](../references/exploration-ship.webp), [Colony ship](../references/colony-ship.webp).

```text
Create one pirate spaceship sprite in the same crisp illustrated mechanical style as the reference fleet, with different colors: heavily weathered rust-red and burnt orange armor, mismatched faded ochre and dirty brown salvage panels, dark charcoal metal and dim warm yellow cockpit windows.
A scrappy makeshift repaired pirate raider with patchwork plates, exposed welded seams, improvised braces, bolted-on mismatched replacement parts, rust and chipped paint. Compact aggressive silhouette, two improvised weapons and mismatched rear engine pods.
Strict orthographic TOP-DOWN view, nose RIGHT, engines LEFT. No perspective or isometric tilt.
Square canvas, complete silhouette, at least 12% transparent padding. True transparent PNG including structural gaps. No scenery, shadow, text, letters, numbers, logos, skull icon, watermark, UI, frame or hex grid. Single ship only. Suggested filename: pirate-ship.png.
```
