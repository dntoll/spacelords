```text
Subject: Salvage ship.

Create a robust industrial recovery tug with two articulated recovery arms, a towing winch and an open working cradle. Strict orthographic TOP-DOWN view, nose pointing RIGHT, engines and tail LEFT. Match the references: painted grey-white armor plates, dark teal structural recesses, turquoise windows and restrained amber accents. Clear compact silhouette, functional science-fiction machinery, crisp detail at small scale. No isometric tilt or perspective. Square canvas.
Background: true transparent PNG with clean alpha, including between structures. No scenery or colored background. Keep the complete silhouette inside the frame with at least 12% clear padding.
Deliver one asset, suggested filename: salvage-ship.png. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, interface elements, borders or hex grid.
```
